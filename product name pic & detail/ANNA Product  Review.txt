6-Wash & Care Instructions
Dry clean recommended to maintain fabric weave and embroidery integrity.
If hand washing, wash gently in cold water with mild detergent.
Do not bleach, wring, or tumble dry.
Line dry in the shade to prevent color fading.
Iron on low-to-medium heat on the reverse side; avoid direct iron contact on embroidered areas.
