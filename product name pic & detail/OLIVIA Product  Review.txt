6-Wash & Care Instructions
Dry clean recommended to preserve fabric richness and intricate threadwork.   
If hand washing, wash gently in cold water using mild detergent.
Wash dark colors separately.Do not bleach, wring, or tumble dry.
Line dry in shade away from direct sunlight.
Iron on low-to-medium heat on the reverse side of embroidery.
