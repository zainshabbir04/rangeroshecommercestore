6-Wash & Care Instructions
Dry clean recommended to preserve fabric quality and detailed embroidery work.
If hand-washing at home, wash gently in cold water with mild liquid detergent.
Wash dark or vibrant colors separately.
Do not bleach, wring, or tumble dry.
Dry in shade to maintain color brilliance.
Iron on low-to-medium heat on the reverse side of embroidered panels.
