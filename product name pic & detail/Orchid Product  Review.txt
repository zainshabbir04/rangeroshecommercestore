6-Wash & Care Instructions
Dry clean recommended to retain fabric richness and delicate cutwork embroidery.   
Hand-wash gently in cold water using mild detergent if washing at home.
Wash dark colors separately; do not bleach, wring, or tumble dry.
Line dry in shade away from direct sunlight.Iron on low-to-medium heat on the reverse side of embroidery.
