6-Wash & Care Instructions
Dry clean recommended to preserve the fabric's texture and intricate embroidery.
If washing at home, hand wash gently in cold water with a mild detergent.
Avoid bleach and harsh chemicals to protect the fabric and embroidery.
Iron at a low temperature, preferably on the reverse side. Avoid direct ironing over embroidered areas.
Do not wring or twist the fabric. Dry in the shade to help maintain its original colour.
