6-Wash & Care Instructions:
Dry clean recommended to retain deep jewel tones and preserve delicate tilla embroidery.   
If hand washing at home, wash gently in cold water using a mild detergent.
Wash dark colors separately; do not bleach, wring, or tumble dry.
Line dry in shade away from direct sunlight.
Iron on low-to-medium heat on the reverse side of embroidered panels.
